## Why this boundary exists

Semi-closed replay owns DAG readiness and recorded tool delays. Replay.Gate sets s.arrivalUs and Request.ArrivalTime before gateway.OnArrival. Do not rewrite ready time at actual dispatch. GatewayHost.Dispatch records separate dispatch time; current native engine then charges admission overhead and registers request before local enqueue. Batch formation owns the only authoritative token, sequence and KV capacity decision. Its continuing requests consume capacity first. Latency is priced on actual batch by external kernel. Policy should own only choice among eligible new requests, not pricing or dependency release.
